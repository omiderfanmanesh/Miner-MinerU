Article 3 - Duration of eligibility for benefits

The benefits offered are granted for the first time for each degree level and, with reference to Higher Technological Institutes, each diploma level. Students already in possession of a qualification, obtained in Italy or abroad, of the same or higher level are therefore not eligible. The level of the degree is determined by universities, conservatories, the Academy of Fine Arts, and Higher Technological Institutes.

University seniority is calculated from the year of first enrolment in the university system, irrespective of any change of course, except for students with disabilities as defined in Article 5 below, for whom it is possible to drop out of studies a maximum of three times.

Freshman' status cannot be granted to students enrolled in the first year, the 2025/2026 academic year, who have waived their studies in years other than the first year in their previous careers or who have applied for credit recognition.

University seniority starts from the year of first enrolment in the university system even in the case of enrolment in three-year degree courses of the new system for students already in possession of a three-year degree from the old system (prior to the 1999 reform).

University seniority is reset, for one time only, in the event of irrevocable withdrawal from studies during the first year, without recognition of examinations (university credits) from the previous career.

In the event of renewed enrolment following a single drop-out in the first year, without recognition of credits, the student is again considered to be enrolled in the regular first year.

In the event of a transfer from another university, or in the event of a request for credit recognition or enrolment with a shortened career, university seniority is calculated from the year of first enrolment at the university or similar institution of origin.

In the event of enrolment in a single-cycle Master's degree course in Law with a shortened career, because the holder holds a Bachelor's degree in "Interlingual Communication Applied to the Legal Professions" and in "Law for Businesses and Institutions", one year is deducted from the university seniority.

In the event of enrolment in courses with programmed access following a change of course, renunciation or transfer from another university, which occurred at the end of the first year of the course, this year is not taken into account for the purposes of calculating seniority.

In the case of recognition by the University of credits from previous careers at Higher Technological Institutes (I.T.S. ACADEMY), university seniority starts from the first year of enrolment at the I.T.S. ACADEMY.

For the purposes of calculating university seniority, the years of enrolment in master's courses for which credit recognition has been requested are taken into account.

The calculation of university seniority excludes the years of enrolment in single courses for which recognition of credits obtained during these courses has not been requested or have not been declared for the purposes of achieving the merit requirement in the online application, as well as the years of university interruption as specified in Article 9, paragraphs 4 and 5, of Legislative Decree 68/2012.

In the event of disqualification, subject to verification with the competent University, the years of actual enrolment shall nevertheless be taken into account in calculating university seniority. Disqualification after only one year of enrolment, without recognition of credits, does not count for the purposes of calculating university seniority.

Article 4 - Eligibility Requirements

To obtain the benefits, students must meet the enrolment, merit, income and assets requirements and any additional specific requirements for each benefit as defined in this call.

4.1 Entry Requirements

Prior enrolment in universities, conservatoires, academies, and higher technological institutes is not required to apply for benefits; however, enrolment must take place within the deadlines set by the individual institutions.

Students who are enrolled at the same time in more than one degree course must notify Ardis by 30 March 2026 by email to ardis@certregione.fvg.it indicating, for each degree course, the University, year of enrolment and

merit. ARDIS will carry out the necessary checks with a view to a possible  $20 \%$ increase in the amount of the scholarship.

For students applying for benefits for an additional semester (undergraduates), the enrolment requirement is deemed to have been met if they were duly enrolled in their final year in the academic year 2024/2025 and obtain their degree by the extraordinary session of the same academic year or if they enrol in the first supplementary year with respect to 2025/2026 by the deadlines set by each institution.

Undergraduates are not eligible for the scholarship if they:

- obtain their title before 18 September 2025  
- graduate in a session of the academic year 2024/2025 and continue their studies for the academic year 2025/2026 at a University, Conservatoire or Institution outside the Friuli Venezia Giulia region (with the exception of Master's degrees).

4.2 Merit requirements

Merit for students is calculated based on regularly registered university credits (CFU) or equivalent.

For Conservatoire and Academy courses read academic credits (CFA) instead of CFU.

For students enrolled in higher technological institutes, the merit requirement is identified by successfully passing the midterm exams of the completed and completed modules.

The following credits are not considered valid for merit and therefore are not taken into account:

> Credits acquired prior to enrolment in their degree course. Exceptions to this are cases of recognition of credits resulting from a transfer from another university or from a change of degree course or career shortening, without prejudice to the assessment of the accumulation of years of university enrolment.  
> Credits arising from career steps taken to clear educational debts from previous careers.  
> Credits arising from career acts relating to individual courses for which recognition of credits obtained during those courses has not been requested or, even if recognised, have not been declared for the purposes of meeting the merit requirement in the online application.  
> Credits arising from career acts relating to didactic activities related to extra exams.

Please note that even in the case of enrolment in a Master's degree with recognition of extra exams deriving from a Bachelor's degree, these credits will not be counted for the purpose of achieving the 20 CFUs as the minimum merit requirement for first-year students to receive the balance of the scholarship  
> Credits arising from integrated examination modules, which are not routinely registered in the career in the Esse3 system until the integrated examination is completed.

However, ARDIS reserves the right to verify special situations relating to administrative procedures concerning students' university careers.

4.2.1 Students enrolled in the first year

Students enrolling for the first time in the first year of the courses, when submitting their online application, do not need any merit requirement for inclusion in the ranking list of the various benefits as eligible and beneficiary.

Students enrolled for the first time in the first year of these courses are granted the first and second instalments of the scholarship based on their fulfilment of the financial condition and enrolment requirements set out in this call.

Merit requirements are assessed on an ongoing basis, for the purposes of retention and payment of the third instalment of the scholarship.

For the purposes of attaining the credits required for the payment of the scholarship, any credits awarded as a result of a waiver or transfer shall not be considered valid and the bonus referred to in Section 4.2.3 may not be used.

Failure to obtain the prescribed credits by 30 November 2026 will result in the revocation of the scholarship and other cash benefits (Art. 24) with the obligation to repay the amount received.

For students enrolled in higher technological institutes, the merit requirement is identified with the successful passing, by August 10, 2025, of the intermediate exams of the completed and completed modules. Possession of this requirement is reported by higher technological institutes to ARDIS.

4.2.3 Bonus

Students enrolled in years subsequent to the first year accrue, depending on the year attended, a bonus in CFUs that can be used to achieve the minimum merit requirement, in addition to the credits actually earned.

The accrued bonus may be:

> Up to 5 credits, if used for the first time to obtain benefits for the second academic year.  
> Up to 12 credits, if used for the first time to obtain benefits for the third academic year.  
> Up to 15 credits, if used for the first time to obtain benefits for the following academic years.

Activation of the bonus takes place:

a. At the applicant's request in an online application.  
b. Ex officio if bonuses are necessary to achieve the minimum merit requirement.

The verification of the actual bonus credits available is done ex officio. Students can check the use of the bonus by accessing their online reserved area of the "Sportello studente" (Student's Desk), in the rankings for the various benefits claimed.

The bonus can only be activated once and cannot be cumulated. The bonus in CFUs refers to the entire university career and does not reset at the end of the individual study cycle. The portion of the bonus accrued but not used remains available for subsequent academic years.

Students who benefited from the bonus in the academic years prior to 2025/2026 may only use the unused portion of the bonus. Bonus credits that may have been claimed in academic years prior to 2025/2026 are not taken into account for the minimum merit requirement under this call.

Students enrolled in master's degree courses or two-year specialisation courses may only use the bonus accrued and not used in the previous three years.

Any unused bonus points can be used to reach the merit requirement for the further semester.

Students must declare any use of bonus credits at other universities in previous careers.

ARDIS reserves the right to carry out a random check on the veracity of the information declared regarding bonus credits used in previous careers, following the publication of the final rankings.

Bonus credits cannot be used to achieve the first-year merit requirement (both Bachelor's and Master's degrees).

4.2.4 Students enrolled in PhD and specialisation courses

Students enrolled in specialisation courses, with the exception of those in the medical area activated based on the regulations in force on the subject and PhD courses that do not benefit from a scholarship from the Universities pursuant to Ministerial Decree no. 224 of 30 April 1999, must be in possession of the requirements laid down by the same for regular enrolment in the current academic year.

4.3 Income and asset requirements

The benefits governed by this call fall under subsidised social benefits. Students' economic conditions are identified based on the "Indicatore della Situazione Economica Equivalente" (ISEE) (Equivalent Financial Situation Indicator) for subsidised benefits related to the right to university education, for 2023 income and assets.

Specifically:

The Equivalent Financial Situation Indicator (ISEE) for subsidised benefits related to the right to university education must not exceed the limit of EUR 27,948.60;  
The Equivalent Asset Indicator (ISPE= ISP/equivalence scale) must not exceed the limit of EUR 60,757.87.

The above conditions must be fulfilled simultaneously. The applicant must therefore have an ISEE value for subsidised benefits related to the right to university education, and at the same time an ISPE value that does not exceed the thresholds indicated above.

4.3.1 Status of students independent of their families of origin

Students are considered independent of their family of origin if they meet both of the following requirements:

$\succ$  Residence established for at least two years with respect to the date of submission of the application for the first time for each course, as evidenced by the personal records, outside the family unit of origin in accommodation not owned by a member of the family unit.  
$\succ$  Presence of an adequate income for at least two years, consisting of tax-declared employment income or assimilated income, not lower than EUR 9,000.00 per year with reference to a household of one person.

In such a case, students may submit an ISEE for subsidised benefits related to the right to university education by setting up their own nucleus.

For the purposes of quantifying the amount of the scholarship, these students are treated in the same way as students with off-premises status.

Disability data are verified with the secretariats of universities, conservatories, the academy and higher technological institutes.
